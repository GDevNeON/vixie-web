A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A little clarity,
before hello.

Vixie is an AI companion experience. You choose how it fits into your day.

What is Vixie?

An AI powered live wallpaper app for Android and Windows. Animated characters respond to touch and conversation.

Is this a real person?

No. The companion is an animated AI character. It can make mistakes and does not have human feelings.

Can I try it before downloading?

Yes. Open the companion demo to try the real Live2D rig, mood controls and sample chat. A configured API enables AI replies.

What devices is it designed for?

Android and Windows. This concept does not advertise an iOS release.

Where can I download it?

The download sketch explains the installation journey. Official release links have not been confirmed.

Does the demo remember me?

Only the current page session is used for chat context. Clear the conversation or reload to start over.

Can I bring my own character?

The planned creator workflow supports model upload, personality, voice, preview and review. Rights and validation checks are part of that process.

Can I turn the motion off?

Yes. Use Motion in the sketch controls. System reduced motion settings take priority; the layout remains readable without animation.
