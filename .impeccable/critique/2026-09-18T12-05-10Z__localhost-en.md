## Overall Impression

A tight, honest bilingual shell. Strongest opportunity: fill the hero's middle void and the sample strip when content phases land — both are owned placeholders, not defects.

## What's Working

1. **Honest placeholder system** — dashed `.dim-*` chips + `aria-disabled` + native `disabled` CTA with reason chip. Users are never tricked by fake links.
2. **Locale parity by construction** — every chrome string via `t()`, switcher preserves path/query/hash, missing routes fall back to the locale homepage.
3. **Status everywhere it matters** — `aria-current` on nav + current locale, live `aria-valuenow` progress, `aria-pressed` theme state, burger `aria-expanded` with Esc + focus return.
